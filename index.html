From painting to permanent record

The last two quests connect your painted system to the real publishing & IP machinery developed in this ecosystem: one metadata record fans out to a permanent DOI + OpenTimestamps proof (misty-doi, publish_paper.sh), and inventions are tracked with OTS disclosure as priority-date evidence (patent_track.sh) — provenance is automated, but every filing decision stays with you and qualified counsel. Open where you can, patent where it counts: the gated open artifact compounds credibility; the tracked disclosure protects the core.
